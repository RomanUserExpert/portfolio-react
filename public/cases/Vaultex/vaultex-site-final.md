## Vaultex — Trading & Portfolio Management Platform

Vaultex is a professional trading platform for active traders and portfolio managers operating across equities, crypto, forex, and commodities. The design challenge was building a product where information density is a requirement rather than a problem to eliminate — time-sensitive decisions, four asset classes, and multiple workflows that needed to function as one coherent system.

---

## Role & Activities

**Competitive Analysis & Domain Research**
Audited professional trading platforms across the complexity spectrum to understand where data density serves experienced users and where it becomes noise. The research established the foundational design principle for the project: high information density is acceptable — ambiguity under time pressure is not. Every layout and hierarchy decision was measured against that standard throughout the project.

**Information Architecture & User Flows**
Structured the product around the natural sequence of a trader's workflow — monitor, manage, execute, assess risk, audit, research — mapping each stage to a dedicated screen with a single primary job. Defined critical task flows to identify where cognitive load peaks and what information must be immediately available versus deferred, establishing the progressive disclosure logic used across the product.

**Interface Design & Iteration**
The core design problem was density management: how much data a screen can carry before it stops being readable under pressure. Ran iterative explorations across layout, typographic hierarchy, spatial grouping, and semantic color use — distinguishing status signals from numerical data visually. Validated decisions against a single test: can a trader extract a meaningful signal from a table in under two seconds.

**Design System & Component Architecture**
Built a component library to support four asset classes, multiple data states, and real-time visual feedback across every screen. Established semantic color tokens, consistent number formatting conventions, and a shared status language that works uniformly across all contexts. Designed for extensibility so new asset classes, order types, and screen types integrate within the established visual and structural language without accumulating inconsistency.

---

## Outcome

A trading platform where the full workflow — from portfolio monitoring to order execution to risk assessment — runs as a continuous, context-preserving flow rather than a set of disconnected screens. Dense financial data reads at a glance rather than requiring interpretation. The design system provides a stable foundation that absorbs product growth without re-solving foundational layout and pattern decisions.
