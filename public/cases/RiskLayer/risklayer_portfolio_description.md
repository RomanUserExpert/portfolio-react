# RiskLayer

`Fintech` `Enterprise SaaS` `Interaction Design`

---

## RiskLayer - Enterprise risk and compliance platform for financial institutions

RiskLayer is a multi-module B2B platform for CROs, Compliance Officers, and Risk Analysts working in fintech and institutional finance. The product spans risk monitoring, stress testing, compliance rule management, exposure tracking, and regulatory audit - six distinct workflows with fundamentally different user goals and information needs. The core design challenge was creating a coherent system across these modules without sacrificing the data density each role requires.

---

## Role & Activities

**Competitive Analysis & Domain Research**
Before any screen work, the competitive landscape was mapped - Moody's RiskIntegrity, AuditBoard, SAS Viya Risk, SS&C Algorithmics. The research revealed a consistent gap: existing tools either prioritize analytical depth at the cost of navigability, or simplify to the point of being unusable for quants. This shaped the strategic direction - institutional density with a navigable structure.

**Information Architecture & Role-Based Navigation**
The platform serves three distinct user roles with different entry points and mental models. IA work centered on resolving the tension between a shared navigation shell and role-specific workflows. The two-group sidebar structure (Monitor / Manage) came directly from mapping how CROs and Compliance Officers move through the product differently - one reacts, the other governs.

**Designing for Data Density and Progressive Disclosure**
The hardest design problem across all six screens was managing information hierarchy in data-heavy contexts - a 5×5 risk matrix with drill-down, a shock parameter table with override states, a compliance condition builder with nested logic. Each screen required a different disclosure strategy: what's visible by default, what appears on interaction, and what lives in a contextual panel.

---

## Outcome

RiskLayer demonstrates a complete enterprise risk platform designed for three distinct professional roles operating under regulatory pressure. Risk managers get an actionable dashboard with drill-down capability. Compliance officers get a no-code rule builder with human-readable logic preview. Analysts get a stress testing environment with scenario comparison and delta analysis. The system holds together visually and structurally across six screens that could otherwise fragment into six unrelated tools.
